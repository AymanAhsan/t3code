# In-app notifications

On web and desktop, notifications appear in arrival order. Open **Notifications** to review waiting items, background job progress, and errors. Dismissing a notification lets the next one appear.

Media downloads run one at a time. If a download fails, its error stays in the notification list after the toast closes. Choose **Retry** to queue another attempt, or **Dismiss** to clear the failure. A failed job does not stop later jobs.

The queue belongs to the current app session. Reloading or closing the app clears it. For mobile push alerts, see [Mobile notifications](mobile-notifications.md).
